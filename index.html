About

Miranda L. Baumann is Director of Research and Academic Publications at the Georgia Department of Behavioral Health and Developmental Disabilities (DBHDD), a role she began in August 2026. Her research examines the intersections of mental health and the justice system, the integration of criminal justice and behavioral health data, and people’s involvement with multiple public systems across the life course. She holds a PhD in Criminal Justice and Criminology from Georgia State University.

Research expertise

  • Justice-related issues in mental health
  • Criminal justice and behavioral health data integration
  • Involvement across multiple public systems over the life course
  • Mental illness and firearm access
  • Child welfare administrative data and permanency outcomes
  • Forecasting in criminal justice policy and practice
