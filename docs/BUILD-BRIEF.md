# Build brief (sanitized)

Summary of the requirements used to build this site. Private planning notes and source documents are not included.

- **Purpose:** explain who Miranda L. Baumann is now, foregrounding her current role and expertise over older history.
- **Audiences:** agency leadership, legislators, research collaborators, and other professional stakeholders. Restrained, accurate language suited to a public-sector role.
- **Boundaries:** personal website only. No agency logos or seals, no implied endorsements, no promises of official services. Footer notice distinguishes the site from the agency.
- **Content sources:** an updated CV supplied by Miranda, with corrections she provided. Publications cross-checked against Crossref/DOI records. Online profiles are not used to override the CV.
- **Scope at launch:** one page — About, Research expertise, Professional background and education, Selected publications (with links to Google Scholar and ORCID), Contact (two email links and LinkedIn). No downloadable CV, blog, contact form, newsletter, chatbot, or content-management system.
- **Excluded:** works in progress, grant applications and funding amounts, phone number, street address.
- **Design:** text only, minimalist, readable, mobile-friendly; neutral colors with one purple accent; light/dark toggle that respects the device setting.
- **Technical:** semantic HTML, minimal JavaScript, accessible navigation and focus states, accurate metadata, sitemap, robots.txt, structured data matching visible content.
- **Process:** content approved before building; preview approved before publishing; every later change previewed and approved before going live.
- **No promises** about search ranking, AI tool visibility, or citations.
